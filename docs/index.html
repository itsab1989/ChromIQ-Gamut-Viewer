Pages the app saved

Every page below was written by ChromIQ Gamut Viewer itself — Save this view as a web page…, nothing edited afterwards. They are here so you can see what that button gives you before installing anything.

They are not pictures. Drag to turn the shape, scroll to zoom, and click a name in the key underneath to hide or show it. Where a page is moving, the strip along the bottom stops it, changes its speed, and switches the two directions of movement on and off.

Each one is a single self-contained file of about 5 MB. The 3D viewer travels inside it, so once it has loaded it needs no internet at all — it works from a memory stick, from an email attachment, or in ten years.
